What we do not assume

Clear scope matters.

Hawk Eyes Cyber Tech does not promise that every failed device can be repaired or that every lost file can be recovered. Outcomes depend on the condition of the equipment, the available evidence and the agreed service scope.

Practical data-protection support is not legal advice. Specialist cybersecurity testing, regulated work or services outside the stated scope must be discussed and agreed before they are represented as available.
