Privacy

How this website handles information.

This page explains the current static website design. It should be reviewed before public launch and updated if hosting, analytics or contact arrangements change.

Consultation form

The consultation form does not submit information to a Hawk Eyes Cyber Tech database. It prepares a message on your device and opens WhatsApp. You decide whether to send that message. WhatsApp processes information under its own terms and privacy arrangements.

Information you should not send

Do not include passwords, payment-card information, authentication codes or other secrets in the consultation form. Hawk Eyes Cyber Tech may request appropriate information later when a service has been discussed and agreed.

Hosting information

The web-hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details and server logs to deliver and protect the website. The final public version of this notice should identify the selected hosting provider and any analytics or cookie tools.

Keeping this notice current

This website currently contains no advertising tracker or custom analytics code. If analytics, embedded maps, payment tools, live chat or another contact processor is added, this notice and any cookie controls should be reviewed.
