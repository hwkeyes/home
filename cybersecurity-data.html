Example 01

How Hawk Eyes Cyber Tech can help

How Hawk Eyes Cyber Tech can help

Common questions

Answers before you book.

If your situation is not covered here, describe the problem by telephone or WhatsApp.

01What should I do if I think my computer has a virus?+

Avoid entering further passwords or payment information, disconnect the device from networks if suspicious activity is continuing, and seek advice before installing multiple unverified cleaning tools.

02Can all deleted data be recovered?+

No. Recovery depends on the storage type, physical condition, what happened after deletion and whether the data has been overwritten. The safest next step is to stop unnecessary use and assess the situation.

03What makes a useful backup?+

A useful backup protects the data that genuinely matters, runs often enough, is stored separately from the main device and can be restored when needed. Simply copying files occasionally may not provide dependable recovery.

04Is this a penetration-testing service?+

The current Hawk Eyes Cyber Tech service focuses on practical device, account, network and data protection. Any specialist testing requirement should be discussed and scoped separately before it is represented as an available service.
