Who we are · Manchester

Practical technology support with a wider view of the problem.

Hawk Eyes Cyber Tech provides computer repairs, networks, CCTV, cybersecurity, data protection and day-to-day business IT support across Manchester and within a 30-mile radius.

Our purpose

Help people understand, repair and protect the technology they rely on.

Technology problems are often connected. A slow computer might involve failing storage, unwanted software or limited memory. A weak network can affect printers, cameras, backups and remote work. A useful service therefore looks beyond the first visible symptom.

Hawk Eyes Cyber Tech brings those connected areas into one support route. We listen to what is happening, assess the likely causes, explain the available options and agree a practical next step.

On-site support is available across Manchester and within a 30-mile radius, with remote support used when it is a suitable and secure route. Availability, timing and the work required are confirmed during the consultation.

01

Understand first

The visible symptom is a starting point. Diagnosis should come before unnecessary replacement or purchasing.

02

Explain clearly

Options, limitations and sensible next steps should be communicated without avoidable technical language.

03

Keep it proportionate

The proposed response should fit the value, condition and intended use of the technology.

04

Consider security

Accounts, updates, backups, access and data risk are considered when they relate to the work.
