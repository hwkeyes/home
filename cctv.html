Illustrative scenarios · not customer case studies

What this service can look like in practice.

These examples show the type of situation Hawk Eyes can help with. The actual solution depends on what the assessment finds.

Example 01

A front entrance is visible but faces are unclear

What's happening

An existing camera shows the whole driveway, yet visitors appear too small or backlit to provide a useful image at the door.

How Hawk Eyes can help

Hawk Eyes can review the purpose, camera angle, distance, lighting and suitable placement so the entrance view is designed to capture the detail actually required.

Example 02

A shop discovers that recent footage is missing

What's happening

The cameras display live images, but the recorder cannot play back the period the owner needs.

How Hawk Eyes can help

We can inspect recording schedules, storage health, date and time settings, retention behaviour and camera connection before explaining the fault and available options.

Example 03

Remote access was shared too widely

What's happening

Several people use the same CCTV login and it is no longer clear who should have access to live or recorded images.

How Hawk Eyes can help

Hawk Eyes can review supported account controls, remove unnecessary access, strengthen credentials and configure individual authorised users where the equipment allows it.

Common questions

Answers before you book.

If your situation is not covered here, describe the problem by telephone or WhatsApp.

01Can you advise where cameras should be positioned?+

Yes. Placement should follow a defined purpose and consider viewing angle, distance, lighting, obstructions, practical installation and privacy responsibilities.

02Can I view CCTV remotely?+

Many current systems support authorised remote viewing. The available options depend on the equipment, internet connection, account controls and security requirements. Remote access should be configured without exposing the system unnecessarily.

03How long will recordings be kept?+

Retention depends on the number of cameras, recording mode, image quality, storage capacity and the customer's legitimate requirement. It should be planned and tested rather than assumed.

04Does CCTV need its own network?+

Not always, but cameras should not simply be added to a business network without thought. Depending on the scale and risk, a separate network segment, controlled access and suitable bandwidth or PoE capacity may be appropriate.
